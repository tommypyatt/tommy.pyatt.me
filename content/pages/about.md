Hi, I'm Tommy: a freelance web developer based in Manchester, UK. I've spent 13+ years building for the web, mostly within ecommerce, and these days that means a mix of Adobe Commerce contract work, small business projects, and building things of my own.

Most of my depth is in the Adobe Commerce (Magento) ecosystem, where I hold the Adobe Commerce Frontend Developer Expert certification. I've worked across everything from legacy Knockout setups to modern Hyvä builds, and Hyvä is now my primary specialism: performant storefronts built with Tailwind CSS and Alpine.js from the ground up.

Modern AI tooling has become a genuine part of how I work day to day, not just something I tinker with. Claude Code in particular is now core to my workflow, and I think being fluent with these tools is fast becoming as important as any other part of the stack. It's changed how quickly I can take something from idea to working software, which is part of what pushed me to found IndieMenu, a commission-free online ordering platform for independent UK restaurants and takeaways, alongside my contract work.

My focus, whatever I'm working on, is clarity and maintainability. I like writing code that makes sense to read, feels good to extend, and quietly gets out of the way once deployed. Whether it's cleaning up a brittle UI or untangling a frontend build, I take pride in leaving a system a little better than I found it. Git keeps that workflow organised and honest: every branch tells the story of what I've learned along the way.

Outside of work you'll usually find me tinkering with something or other: setting up a retro gaming machine, experimenting with audio gear, or pulling apart the latest local AI tooling. I'm genuinely passionate about open-source and Linux, and most of my experiments start in a terminal window on some distro I've broken and fixed a few times. I like understanding how things work, breaking them occasionally, and learning from the process.

If you're looking to work together, you can find my full CV and availability on my [CV page](/cv/).
